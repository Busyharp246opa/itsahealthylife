# Healthy Life visual system

The Next.js app uses the original It’s A Healthy Life identity from the supplied landing kit. The logo is stored in `public/brand/`, with the Host and Bricolage font files. The public site remains the original Healthy Life landing and About design in `public/landing/`.

The workspace palette follows `tokens.css`: plum `#52305c`, lime `#75d100`, lavender `#f3e8f9`, warm white `#f7f4f8`, and ink `#211725`. `app/focus-circle.css` applies the kit to the imported workspace layouts, controls, dashboard, and social home. The dark plum shell supports bright lime actions; light task surfaces keep long forms and tables readable.

The FitCircle implementation supplies the feature structure and interaction patterns. The Healthy Life brand supplies visible names, logos, fonts, palette, and imagery. FitLoop and the TDEE calculator are outside this product. Original concept rationale is retained under `Sample UI/` and the previous Vite UI under `legacy/healthy-life-vite/`.
