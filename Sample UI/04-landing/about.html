THE THINKING BEHIND HEALTHY LIFE

More than a workout.

It’s a Healthy Life began with a wider view of wellbeing: exercise, accessible food ideas, useful expert guidance and people who help each other keep going. Its original website describes a service developed in London for people across the UK.

Today’s web platform brings those parts into one place. Members can shape a training week, log everyday activity and meals, discover experts and take part in the Circle. Trainers can apply to be verified, manage sessions and support booked clients.

There is no perfect starting point. Begin with the part of your day you can change, then build a rhythm that feels like yours.
