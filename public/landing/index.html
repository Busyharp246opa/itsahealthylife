04 / GOOD TO KNOW

A little clarity
goes a long way.

More about Healthy Life is on our story page.

Do I need a trainer to get started?+

No. You can build your own weekly training programs, log meals and review progress, and explore the community. Expert support is there when you want it.

Can I use Healthy Life on my phone?+

Yes. The member and trainer workspaces are responsive web experiences that work on phone and desktop browsers.

How does a trainer join?+

Create an account, complete the expert application, and submit your credentials for review. Approved experts can then use the full trainer workspace.
